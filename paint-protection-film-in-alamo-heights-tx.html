How dark can my window tint legally be in Texas?
In Texas, the front side windows must allow at least 25% Visible Light Transmission (VLT). The rear side windows and back windshield can be tinted to any darkness level provided the vehicle has dual exterior side mirrors. On the front windshield, tint is permitted above the AS-1 line or along the top 5 inches.
What is the difference between ceramic tint and regular dyed tint?
Regular dyed tint primarily darkens glass for glare reduction and privacy, but offers limited thermal rejection and can fade or turn purple over time. Ceramic tint uses microscopic non-metallic ceramic particles to block over 90% of infrared heat and 99% of harmful UV rays without interfering with cellular or GPS signals, providing superior lifelong heat rejection.
How long does window tint take to cure in San Antonio's climate?
In San Antonio's warm climate, window tint typically takes between 3 to 5 days to fully cure. During cooler or humid winter months, curing may take up to 2 weeks. It is essential to keep all windows rolled up and avoid cleaning the inner glass surfaces until all residual moisture has fully evaporated.
Will window tinting interfere with my GPS, toll tag, or phone signals?
No. Alamo Auto Aesthetics exclusively installs high-performance ceramic and carbon window films that are 100% non-metallic. They will not disrupt cellular signals, Bluetooth connectivity, satellite navigation systems, or your TxTag electronic toll transponder.
Does window tinting help improve fuel economy or EV range?
Yes. By blocking massive amounts of solar infrared heat from entering your cabin, premium window tinting keeps your vehicle significantly cooler. This allows your air conditioning system to cool the interior much faster and run with less compressor strain, reducing fuel consumption in gas vehicles and preserving battery range in electric vehicles.
